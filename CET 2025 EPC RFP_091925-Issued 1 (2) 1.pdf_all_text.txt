[ParagraphRole.SECTION_HEADING] 1. Purpose of the RFP

[None] Con Edison Transmission, Inc. (CET) issues this Request for Proposal (RFP) to identify and prequalify strategic firms in the engineering and construction sector who are interested in supporting CET's development of electric transmission projects from origination through project execution and delivery. As noted in Section 9 below, this is Phase 1 of the RFP process, and our intention is to shortlist firms for one or more subsequent rounds of qualifications and/or proposals as we further develop our vision for this procurement and relationship.

[None] Our intention is to engage development teams who will help grow CET's transmission investment portfolio, by enhancing CET's competitiveness in transmission procurements in our target markets. We envision that support would start with preconstruction and/or a teaming approach during early phase project development, including support of planning, scheduling, scoping, engineering, routing, construction, cost estimating and risk analysis. We would also seek support to develop cash flow estimates and operating cost models corresponding to a project milestone schedule. In addition, we seek support in preparing written proposals in response to solicitations in which CET participates.

[None] CET also envisions continuing with selected firms past the project and proposal development phase and into execution phases of selected projects. CET is interested in exploring collaborative risk sharing contracting models, and/or incentive option structures with strategic engineering and construction firms to deliver on selected projects. For instance, if CET develops a proposal with a strategic engineering and construction firm, and the proposal is competitively awarded to CET, we envision continuing with that engineering / construction firm through project execution, in a collaborative contracting model with appropriate risk sharing and/or incentive structures, subject to appropriate parameters to be determined.

[None] We are seeking interest from firms who are willing to team with CET and share in the risk and value of project delivery in this fashion and are asking for input from market players on what options or arrangements might be offered or recommended in exchange for priority opportunities to work with CET to deliver a selected project.

[ParagraphRole.SECTION_HEADING] 2. About CET

[None] CET, a subsidiary of Consolidated Edison, Inc. (CEI), is a transmission development company active in electric transmission development in the New York, PJM and New England markets, with a focus on FERC regulated transmission opportunities, both regionally and inter-regionally. CET expects to be increasingly active in these markets to capture the significant potential for transmission expansion needed to meet increasing demand and new generation supplies.

[None] CET is affiliated with Consolidated Edison Company of New York, Inc. and Orange and Rockland Utilities, Inc. with approximately $15 billion in revenue and $72 billion in assets.
